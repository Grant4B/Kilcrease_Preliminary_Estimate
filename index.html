Scope of Work

What We're Building

Below is a breakdown by project. The rear addition with its ensuite and the building systems form the core scope. The front driveway, basement, and garage projects are optional add ons you can include in any combination.

01

Rear Addition, Deck, Hardscape & New Ensuite Bathroom

Addition + Kitchen

We'll build a two story rear addition of roughly 200 square feet of footprint, about 400 square feet of new conditioned space across the two floors. The ground floor enlarges and reworks the kitchen; the second floor adds a new bedroom served by a new ensuite bathroom. Tying the new space into the home will require some reconfiguration of the existing second floor, which we'll detail during design. We'll also rebuild the existing deck at grade and add hardscape to transition gracefully up the sloped rear yard.

  • New two story rear addition: foundation, framing, weather tight envelope, roofing, siding, and windows to complement the existing home
  • Ground floor footprint opened into the existing kitchen, with a reworked layout, semi custom cabinetry, stone countertops, and a mid range, high quality appliance allowance
  • Structural work to open the existing rear wall, with beam sizing confirmed during design
  • Second floor bedroom with new flooring, drywall, trim, paint, closet, and full electrical
  • Reconfiguration of part of the existing second floor (walls, doorways, or hallway) to connect the new bedroom and ensuite cleanly
  • New ensuite bathroom serving the bedroom, with a walk in shower and glass enclosure, vanity with stone top, tile floor and walls, and mid range, high quality plumbing fixtures
  • Reconfiguration of the existing second floor (walls, doorways, and circulation) as needed to integrate the new bedroom and ensuite
  • Heating, cooling, plumbing, and electrical runs extended to serve the new space, including net new supply and drain runs for the ensuite
  • Rebuild of the existing grade level deck with new framing, decking, and railings
  • Hardscape transition to the uphill rear yard: retaining elements, steps, and a patio or landing as needed for grade
  • DC permit coordination, zoning review, and structural engineering for the addition
02

Building Systems

Systems

As part of the core scope, we'll bring the home's mechanical systems up to date alongside the addition. Based on what we discussed, the HVAC and water heater are near the end of their service life, and the electrical panel needs to be upgraded to support the added load from the new space and any further work.

  • Replacement HVAC system, sized for the home and the newly conditioned space
  • New water heater
  • Electrical panel and service upgrade or replacement to support new circuits and capacity
  • Permits and inspections for the mechanical and electrical work
  • Sized to carry any of the optional projects below without a second round of mechanical work
03

Front Driveway & Retaining Wall (Option)

Sitework

At the front of the property, we'll demolish and rebuild one retaining wall, relocating it to widen the driveway, and then repave the driveway surface.

  • Demolition and removal of the existing retaining wall
  • New retaining wall, shifted to widen the drive
  • Excavation, drainage, and backfill as required for the relocated wall
  • Repaving of the driveway surface
  • Engineering for the wall confirmed during design if height or soil conditions require it
04

Basement Laundry, Mudroom & Full Bath (Option)

Lower Level

This option remodels roughly 200 square feet of the basement into a dedicated laundry room and mudroom, and reinstates a full bathroom in the location where an original two piece bathroom was removed. Together they create a hard working lower level that takes the daily load off the rest of the house. We'll plan around the existing rough in where possible and confirm below slab plumbing during design.

  • Dedicated laundry room with cabinetry, counter, utility sink, and washer and dryer hookups
  • Mudroom with built in lockers or cubbies, a bench, and durable flooring
  • New full bath with tub and tile shower surround, keeping a tub on the lower level for flexibility
  • Vanity, toilet, mid range high quality fixtures, and tile floor and walls in the bath
  • Plumbing supply and drainage for laundry and bath, with below slab work confirmed during design (allowance carried for unknowns)
  • Drywall, trim, paint, finish carpentry, updated electrical, GFCI protection, ventilation, and lighting
  • Basement conditions (moisture, slab, ceiling height) reviewed during design
05

Garage Conversion to Multipurpose Space (Option)

Conversion

This option converts the small existing garage into a conditioned, multipurpose space, usable as a studio, office, gym, or flex room. We'll insulate, finish, and bring the space up to a comfortable, code compliant condition.

  • Insulation of walls, roof or ceiling, and slab perimeter, with air sealing for comfort and efficiency
  • New interior framing, drywall, flooring, trim, and paint
  • New exterior door and window in place of the garage door, to suit the intended use
  • Electrical circuits, lighting, and heating and cooling tied to the building systems
  • Existing slab, foundation, and roof condition reviewed during design

How We Work

Our Design + Build Process

We're not a contractor who shows up with a hammer on day one. We invest in the planning phase because that's where great projects are won or lost. Here's what the journey looks like from here.

1

Design Development & Drawings

We'll develop floor plans, elevations, and 3D views, and help you decide which projects to advance and in what order. The flexible scope here means you can phase the work, starting with the addition and ensuite, for example, and adding the lower level options later.

2

Selection & Specification

We guide you through all finish and fixture selections including cabinetry, tile, countertops, plumbing fixtures, lighting, and hardware, keeping you within your budget with clear allowances and upgrade paths.

3

Permitting & Pre-Construction

We handle all permit applications with DC, coordinate structural engineering for the addition and the retaining wall, and finalize your construction contract with confirmed pricing before any work begins.

4

Construction & Regular Communication

Your dedicated project manager will keep you updated weekly, and often daily, on progress, decisions needed, and scheduling. We work clean, communicate proactively, and respect your home throughout construction.

5

Final Walkthrough & Closeout

We don't consider a project complete until you do. A formal punch list walkthrough ensures every detail is addressed before we close out, and we remain available afterward for any warranty questions.

Design Services

Design Phase Investment

The design phase is a separate, dedicated engagement, and the foundation everything else is built on. Here's how it's structured, with budgeting estimates shown for the core scope and the full scope so you can plan whether to take on everything or phase it.

How Design Fees Work

Design services are billed hourly and scoped to your project. They are not included in the construction estimate. As a general budgeting guideline, design fees typically run approximately 10% of the estimated construction cost.

  • Senior Staff Principal designer / project lead
    $200 / hr
  • Associate Staff Design associate / drafter
    $150 / hr
This is a budgeting estimate, not a fixed fee. Your actual design cost depends on the complexity of decisions, number of revision rounds, and scope changes during design. We'll provide a detailed scope of design services in your agreement before any work begins. Fees are billed in installments against a retainer rather than all at once, and any unearned balance is returned if you direct the project to stop. Design and construction are billed separately, so the design fee is its own engagement and does not apply to the construction contract.
